1. Executive Overview

JusticeFlow is a modern case management software designed specifically for law firms, legal clinics, and corporate legal departments. In standard practice, law firms suffer from disconnected tools: emails scattered across inboxes, court pleadings lost in desktop folders, missed filing deadlines, and untracked billable hours that lead to revenue leakage.

JusticeFlow integrates the entire lifecycle of legal practice into a unified, secure operating system:

  • Retainer Management: Maintain verified contact profiles for both corporations and individuals.
  • Matter & Docket Tracking: Track every lawsuit, arbitration, patent defense, and corporate filing through dedicated stages.
  • Budget Guardrails: Proactively monitor legal expenses against client-approved budget caps to prevent billing disputes.
  • Privileged Document Repository: Centralized digital filing cabinet for pleadings, motions, and evidence exhibits.
  • Contemporary Time Capture: Log billable hours in real-time with automated rate calculations and realization reporting.

2. System Architecture in Simple Terms

JusticeFlow is built using an independent microservice architecture. Instead of one large monolithic software where a single failure halts the entire firm, JusticeFlow separates duties into 6 dedicated services coordinated through an API Gateway:

Service Component Port Primary Responsibility Why This Matters to the Firm
API Gateway 5000 Traffic director & security checkpoint Single point of entry that routes all client and lawyer requests securely.
Auth Service 3001 Staff identities & passwords Guarantees that only authorized personnel can see confidential case records.
Client Service 3002 Client CRM & retainers Organizes corporate clients, billing addresses, and assigned partner lawyers.
Case Service 3003 Legal matters & court dockets Maintains case numbers, court branches, assigned judges, and approved budgets.
Documents Service 3004 File storage & downloads Stores legal pleadings, briefs, exhibits, and contracts with download links.
Time Tracking Service 3005 Billable hours & fee realization Captures attorney work slips and automatically updates case budget utilization.

3. End-to-End Legal Practice Workflow

The system mirrors the actual day-to-day legal workflow in five seamless stages:

  1. Client Retainer: A new corporate entity or individual retains the firm. Their profile, jurisdiction, and lead counsel are saved in the Client Directory.
  2. Matter Inception: A specific matter is opened (e.g. Commercial Breach of Contract) with an assigned docket number, court, judge, and client budget.
  3. Evidence & Pleadings: Complaint files, contracts, and filings are uploaded to the matter file in the Documents Repository.
  4. Legal Service & Time Slips: Attorneys and paralegals record work performed (e.g., 3.5 hours drafting deposition questions at $450/hour).
  5. Financial Burn-Down: The matter's budget is automatically recalculated and visually tracked on the Executive Dashboard.

4. Detailed Page-by-Page User Guide

Screen 1: Secure Portal Authentication (`/login`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/login

Purpose: The front door of the firm. Protects all privileged legal information.

  • Sign-In & Register: Toggle between logging into an existing account or registering new staff.
  • Password Encryption: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed before storing in the database.
  • One-Click Demo Buttons: Instant credentials autofill for quick login during evaluations.

Screen 2: Executive Practice Dashboard (`/dashboard`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/dashboard

Purpose: The cockpit for managing partners and legal teams to inspect business health in seconds.

  • Active Matters Card: Shows how many cases are currently in discovery or trial.
  • Retained Clients Card: Shows the active retained client base.
  • Billable Hours Card: Real-time count of all hours worked across the firm.
  • Billed Realization Card: Total fee revenue earned from client work slips.
  • Active Matters Table: Features color-coded progress bars showing how much of each case's budget has been consumed.
  • Court Deadlines Calendar: Displays upcoming court appearances, motions, and trial dates.

Screen 3: Client Master Directory (`/clients`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/clients

Purpose: Complete CRM for managing client retainers, corporate representatives, and conflict checks.

  • Client Records: Shows corporate name, contact email, phone, location, and assigned lead attorney.
  • Matters Counter: Shows the number of active lawsuits linked to each client.
  • Add/Edit Modal: Retain a new client or update existing contact details.
  • Quick Search & Status Filter: Instant search by name, city, or phone number.

Screen 4: Legal Matters & Cases (`/cases`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/cases

Purpose: The core docket manager tracking all lawsuits, arbitrations, and transactions.

  • Practice Area Filters: Filter by Commercial Litigation, Corporate Securities, Intellectual Property, Probate, or Real Estate.
  • Status Badges: Open (in discovery), Pending (in trial), On Hold (settlement stay), Closed (resolved).
  • Budget Meter: Visual green/yellow/red bar indicating financial burn.
  • New Matter Modal: Allows entry of court jurisdiction, judge, docket number, and approved budget.

Screen 5: 360° Case Detail & File Dossier (`/cases/:id`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/cases/1

Purpose: The complete virtual trial binder for a specific lawsuit.

  • Financial Health Box: Shows exact budget spent versus approved cap with percentage.
  • Court & Client Cards: Instant access to presiding judge, court branch, and client contact info.
  • Tab 1 — Legal Documents: Lists all complaints, motions, and contracts attached to this case with direct download links.
  • Tab 2 — Logged Hours: Complete itemized ledger of every attorney time slip recorded for this matter.
  • Tab 3 — Factual Synopsis: Strategic notes and factual background of the case.

Screen 6: Legal Documents & Evidence Repository (`/documents`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/documents

Purpose: Centralized law firm filing cabinet preventing misplaced files.

  • Classification Filter: Pleadings, Contracts, Exhibits, Orders, Term Sheets, and Settlement Agreements.
  • Document Upload Modal: Upload real PDF and DOCX attachments linked to any legal matter.
  • Download Stream: Securely stream documents to your computer.

Screen 7: Attorney Time Tracking & Billing (`/time-tracking`)

URL: http://localhost:4200/time-tracking

Purpose: The revenue engine of the law firm.

  • Revenue Metrics: Total billable hours and billed realization across all matters.
  • Log Time Slip Modal: Input matter, hours, hourly rate ($/hr), and narrative description of legal tasks performed.
  • Automated Budget Sync: As soon as hours are logged, the system automatically recalculates the matter's total spent budget.

6. Security, Compliance & Data Integrity

  • SQL Injection Immunity: All database operations utilize parameterized prepared queries through the mysql2/promise pool.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Authenticated requests carry digital JWT signatures verifying staff permissions.
  • Tamper-Resistant Audit Trail: Critical operations (matter creation, time slip recording, file deletion) are logged into the audit_logs database table.
