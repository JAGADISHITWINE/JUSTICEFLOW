## Introduction

JusticeFlow is a simple, all-in-one software program that helps legal offices manage clients, court cases, documents, and billing in one safe place. This friendly guide walks you through every daily task step by step. You do not need any technical experience to use this software with total confidence.

---

## Before You Start

Please make sure you have the following ready before you begin:
- A computer, laptop, or tablet connected to the internet.
- A modern web browser such as Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Mozilla Firefox.
- The web address for JusticeFlow (`http://localhost:4200`).
- Your login email address and password.

## Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

### Question 1: I typed my email and password, but I cannot sign in. What should I check?
- **Answer**: First, check that your **Caps Lock** key is turned off on your keyboard. Second, make sure there are no accidental spaces before or after your email address. Third, if you are testing the system, click one of the quick autofill buttons at the bottom of the login screen to sign in automatically.

### Question 2: Why is the budget bar on my case turning red?
- **Answer**: The budget bar turns green when you have used less than 70% of your approved budget. It turns yellow between 70% and 90%. When the total cost of your logged hours exceeds 90% of your budget, the bar turns red as a friendly reminder to discuss fees with your client.

### Question 3: Can a client see our private attorney research or internal notes?
- **Answer**: No. The Client Portal is completely separate from the internal staff tools. Clients can only see finalized court documents, hearing dates, and invoices that you choose to share with them. All internal strategy notes remain private.
